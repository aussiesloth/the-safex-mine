# Art and Audio Direction - Open Decisions

## Visual direction

Two primary directions remain deliberately open for testing:

1. **Old-school pixel / 8-bit-inspired mine**
   - strong retro-game personality;
   - simple sprite animation;
   - naturally tolerant of exaggerated effects.

2. **Smoother modern 2D illustration**
   - cleaner character motion;
   - richer dust/light effects;
   - potentially broader appeal.

Prototype both before locking the art style.

## Required scene elements

- active rock face;
- animated miner;
- lantern/light source;
- reward tables/area;
- chair for stopped/disconnected/crashed states;
- fool's-gold/reject location;
- space for high-volume consolidated rewards.

## Sound

Sound is part of the intended experience but remains to be designed.

Potential cues:
- normal pick impacts;
- accepted-block reveal/celebration;
- reject/fool's-gold crumble or dull clink;
- streak escalation;
- subtle connection/failure cue.

A clearly visible **Mute** button on the main screen is mandatory.

## Asset rule

Every committed art/audio/font asset must have documented provenance and a licence that permits the intended distribution and modification.
